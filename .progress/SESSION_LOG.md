## 2026-10-10 — withdrawal approval workflow (hand-written note)

A user cannot move funds out unreviewed. They **request** a withdrawal (amount reserved
from the spendable balance), and an admin decides it at `/admin/withdrawals`.

- Declining **returns the amount to the requesting user** with the reason recorded; nothing
  in the codebase can divert a user's reservation to another account. Declining is never a
  forfeiture, and no path sweeps a user's balance to an operator wallet.
- Approving settles the app-signable rail (compliance screening → spend caps →
  custody-signed transfer) or clears the Coinbase hand-off, whose real debit arrives later
  as a reconciled webhook. A payout refused **before** signing is refunded; one that may
  have been broadcast keeps its funds held until an admin verifies the chain and releases.
- An admin can place an **account hold**: withdrawals and trades freeze, with a required
  reason, an audit entry and a user notification. The balance is untouched and lifting the
  hold restores access.
- Schema: `WithdrawalRequest` + `Wallet.blocked/blockedReason/blockedAt` +
  `Transaction.ref`; migration `20261010140000_withdrawal_requests` (SQLite + Postgres).
- Coverage: `tests/integration/withdrawal-requests.test.ts` (19) and
  `tests/e2e/withdrawal-approval.spec.ts` (3).
- **Not built, on purpose:** any automatic transfer of declined or held user funds to
  `0x5a40…Bf8c` or any other operator address. Freezing funds and owning them are different
  powers.

## 2026-10-10 — dispute window, account ban, and the funds-stay-with-the-user line (hand-written note)

Follow-up to the withdrawal workflow: a decline now opens a bounded argument in support,
and confirmed misuse bans the account. **Still no code path moves money on a decline, a
hold or a ban.**

- **Dispute window.** A declined request records `disputeOpensAt`/`disputeClosesAt` — a
  business-day window (`DISPUTE_WINDOW_BUSINESS_DAYS`, default 3, Mon–Fri UTC; no
  public-holiday calendar, and documented as a convention rather than a legal deadline).
  The user is told the exact closing date and argues it in a support thread
  (`openDisputeTicket`) assigned to the admin who declined, so thread and decision stay
  together. The window expiring is not a decision.
- **Resolution.** `resolveDisputeAction` is admin-only and has exactly two outcomes and
  neither moves money: `CLEARED` closes the case with the refusal standing; `MISUSE_CONFIRMED`
  **bans** the account (`User.banned` — sign-in refused and existing sessions treated as
  signed out) and **freezes** the balance (`Wallet.blocked`), with a required reason, audit
  entries (`account.banned`, `withdrawal.disputeMisuseConfirmed`) and a user notification.
  The frozen balance stays the account holder's.
- **Ban is access control, hold is movement control, neither is ownership.** `setAccountBanAction`
  is refused for ADMIN/owner accounts (`?ban=protected`). A banned wallet is refused at SIWE
  sign-in (`isWalletBanned`) and `getCurrentUser` returns null for it, invalidating any live
  session. `/admin/withdrawals` has no destination input anywhere in the hold/ban/dispute paths.
- **Schema:** `User.banned/bannedReason/bannedAt` +
  `WithdrawalRequest.disputeOpensAt/disputeClosesAt/disputeOutcome`; migration
  `20261010160000_dispute_window_and_ban` (SQLite rebuild + Postgres), alongside
  `20261010120000_wallet_defaults_mainnet` and `20261010140000_withdrawal_requests`.
- **Coverage:** `tests/withdrawals-window.test.ts` (15) and
  `tests/integration/withdrawal-requests.test.ts` (26, incl. bans, holds and dispute
  outcomes) plus `tests/e2e/withdrawal-approval.spec.ts` (3).
- **Not built, on purpose:** any automatic transfer of a declined, held or banned user's
  balance to `0x5a40…Bf8c` or any other operator address. A decline returns the amount, a
  hold/ban freezes it, and taking it is a separate documented, adjudicated process — not a
  side effect of a decision form.
