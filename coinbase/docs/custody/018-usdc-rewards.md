# USDC Rewards

> Earn USDC rewards on qualifying balances held in CDP wallets.

## Non-custodial Wallet Rewards

<Info>
  **New:** USDC Rewards are now available on **Solana**, in addition to
  supported EVM networks.
</Info>

Eligible developers can earn up to **3.60% USDC rewards** by holding qualifying USDC balances in CDP non-custodial wallets on supported chains including Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism, Avalanche, and Solana.

Currently, rewards are available to US-based individuals and businesses who maintain an average daily balance of at least **1 USDC** over a given week. Rewards are paid weekly in USDC to the developer's Coinbase Retail or Coinbase Business account.

<Tip>
  To receive USDC Rewards, you must have previously held a USDC balance in your
  Coinbase (Retail or Business) account.
</Tip>

### USDC rewards details

* USDC Rewards is a Coinbase-funded loyalty program for eligible developers.
* Rewards apply to qualifying balances held in CDP wallets on supported EVM networks and on Solana.
* Coinbase has no right to use USDC held in CDP wallets.
* Coinbase may change or discontinue the USDC Rewards program with reasonable notice.
