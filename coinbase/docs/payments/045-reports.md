# Reports

> What's included in a Payment Acceptance report, and how to get one today.

Payment Acceptance reports export transaction-level data for reconciliation, compliance, and financial reporting.

<Note>
  Payment Acceptance reports aren't yet available through the CDP Portal or API. [Contact our team](mailto:coinbase.payments@coinbase.com) to get recurring reports set up for your account.
</Note>

## What's in a Payment Acceptance report

Reports include transaction-level data for captures, refunds, voids, and disbursements processed through your Payment Acceptance integration. Key data:

* Payment session and transaction identifiers
* Amounts, assets, and settlement details
* Fee breakdowns
* Buyer source (wallet address or Coinbase account)
* Timestamps for each lifecycle event
* Your metadata and external reference IDs

## Getting started

[Contact our team](mailto:coinbase.payments@coinbase.com) to set up recurring reports for your account.
